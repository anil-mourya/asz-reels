# Voice-over — Diwali v2 (≈ 26 s, read slowly, like talking to a society secretary)

Record on the phone's voice recorder, quiet room (no fan/AC), phone ~20 cm from your mouth,
one take, leave 1 s silence at the start. "…" = a natural half-second pause.

| time | line (Hinglish) | Devanagari |

| 2.6–5.4 | Hamari team har floor pe jaake check karti hai. | हमारी टीम हर फ्लोर पे जाके चेक करती है। |

Also record 10 s of silence at one site ("room tone") if you can — it smooths the cuts.

## Technical wording review
- Gauge check applies to stored-pressure extinguishers (ABC powder, water, foam). CO2 and
  cartridge types have no gauge — they are checked by weight — so s3 must show a gauge unit.
- "Refilled this year?" (old v1 card) is dropped: refilling is due after use, on pressure/weight
  loss, or at the interval in IS 2190, not every year. The reel now asks "Service date valid hai?"
- Shaft/duct doors on electrical and plumbing shafts are kept closed to stop smoke and fire
  spreading floor to floor — correct as worded.
- Refuge areas and staircases must be kept free of storage — correct as worded.
- "Free site inspection" and "AMC quote in 30 minutes" removed pending the owner's confirmation.
